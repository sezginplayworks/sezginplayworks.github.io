Privacy Policy

Boulder Panic · Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Boulder Panic (the “Game”), published by Sezgin PlayWorks (“we”, “us”), handles information. We do not require you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal details inside the Game.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game stores a small amount of data only on your device: your best score, your recent high scores and your sound setting. This data does not leave your device unless it is sent to a leaderboard as described below. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the Game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve ad quality, Google may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), only where permitted;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system, language and app information;
  • interactions with ads (for example, ad views and taps) and diagnostic data.

Your choices: On iOS, the Game asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. If you do not allow tracking or personalized ads, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Leaderboards (Game Center and Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), your best score is sent to the leaderboard together with your player profile (such as your nickname and player ID) provided by Apple or Google. Leaderboards are operated by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out of these services in your device settings.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run our own servers that collect data from the Game.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game. Information collected by Google, Apple or other third parties is kept according to their policies. If you would like help with a data request, contact us at the email below.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Turkish KVKK or California privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, most requests are handled through the settings described above or directly with Google or Apple. We are happy to help — just contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact
