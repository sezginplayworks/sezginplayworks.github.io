Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap and hold the left or right side of the screen to move your miner. Dodge the falling boulders — every boulder you dodge is 1 point and makes your miner a little bigger.

What do the walnuts do?

Brown walnuts shrink your miner. Black walnuts instantly make your miner giant — avoid them!

Can I continue after getting hit?

Once per game you can watch a short ad to continue from your current score.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the SOUND button in the top-right corner of the main screen or the game over screen.

Why don't I see the world ranking?

Sign in to Game Center (iOS: Settings › Game Center) or Google Play Games (Android). Your best score is sent automatically the next time you open the game with an internet connection.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. You can play without an internet connection; ads and leaderboards need a connection.

I reinstalled the game and lost my best score.

Scores are stored on your device, so uninstalling removes them. Your best score on the world ranking stays on Game Center / Google Play Games.
